A desk, not a platform

Less friction.
More doing.

This is my personally hosted collection of tools for creative work, practical thinking, and the small jobs that interrupt both.

Everything calculates in your browser. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or remote processing. Text and JSON stay in this tab; pins, decisions, references, and the focus timer use this browser’s local storage. They do not sync between devices.

Export anything you want to keep. Use the reference shelf to build your own collection. Press / to find a tool.

Take your desk with you

One backup holds your pins, matrix, references and focus intention. Text and JSON drafts stay out of it. Imported timers resume paused.

This removes only this desk’s pins, decision matrix, reference shelf, and focus timer.
